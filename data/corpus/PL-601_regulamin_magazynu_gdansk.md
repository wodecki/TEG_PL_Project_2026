# Gdańsk Warehouse Rules

## Working hours

The Gdańsk warehouse runs three shifts, Monday to Saturday. Supplier deliveries are received between 6:00 and 14:00. Goods issue for delivery routes starts at 4:30.

## Personal protective equipment

Everyone on the warehouse floor must wear a high-visibility vest and safety shoes with toe caps. In the cold store, thermal clothing is also required, and continuous work in the cold store may not exceed 90 minutes without a break to warm up.

## Forklifts

Forklifts may be operated only by people with valid UDT certification. The maximum speed is 10 km/h on the warehouse floor and 5 km/h in the picking area. Before every shift, the operator checks the forklift and records the check in the log.

## Visitors and external drivers

Visitors may enter the warehouse site only accompanied by a Kovalo employee, after signing the visitor book and receiving an ID badge. Visitors must complete a short health and safety briefing and are given a high-visibility vest. External drivers stay in the designated area by the loading docks and do not enter the picking hall.

## Prohibitions

Smoking is strictly prohibited anywhere on the warehouse site, except at the designated spot by the entrance gate. Using a mobile phone while driving a forklift is prohibited.

## Incidents

Every accident, damage to goods or failure of refrigeration equipment must be reported to the shift leader immediately.
